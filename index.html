About me

      
I'm a student from Tanauan City, Batangas who builds hardware prototypes with Arduino. I like the moment a project first moves on its own: a servo aiming at a flame, a pump pouring water, a machine dropping an item after a coin goes in. My builds use flame sensors, coin acceptors, servos, relays, water pumps and LCDs.

      
I like taking a project from idea to wiring to working demo. I debug one part at a time until the whole system runs.
